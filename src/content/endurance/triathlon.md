title: Triathlon

summary: >-
  Competing as a triathlete alongside ultra-distance cycling — swim, bike and
  run, and the training discipline of holding three sports at once.
order: 5
---

Triathlon is the counterweight to ultra-cycling. Where an ultra is a single
sustained effort, a triathlon is three disciplines that each punish neglect of
the other two. Training for both forces a schedule that has to be planned
properly or it simply falls apart.
