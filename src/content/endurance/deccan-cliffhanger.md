The Deccan Cliffhanger is the closest thing India has to a proving ground for
ultra-distance cycling: 646 kilometres, Pune to Goa, ridden straight through
with a support crew and an unforgiving time limit.

What an ultra actually tests is not fitness. It is the quality of your planning
under fatigue — nutrition timing, crew handoffs, when to push and when to
protect the legs for the climbs still ahead. It is the same skill as running an
operation, compressed into thirty-odd hours.
