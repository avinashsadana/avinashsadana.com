Beyond organised racing, most of the riding has been self-planned: routes
chosen, logistics solved, and then simply ridden.

**Vadodara – Kutch – Vadodara** crosses one of the emptiest landscapes in
western India and back. **The Sabarmati-to-Dandi Salt Ride** retraces the route
of the 1930 Salt March — history under the wheels rather than in a book.
