Heavy engineering rewards people who can hold two things at once: the commercial
picture and the physical reality of what is being built. This role sat exactly
there — between the suppliers who cut and roll the steel, and the sales teams
selling into markets on the other side of the world.

The Procure-to-Pay project was the more instructive of the two. Most of the delay
in a procurement cycle is not in the negotiation; it is in the waiting between
steps. Making that visible was most of the work.
