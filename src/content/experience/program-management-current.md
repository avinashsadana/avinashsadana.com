Power infrastructure is an unglamorous, essential business, and a startup
building it has to solve two problems at once: make the product, and invent the
operating rhythm that lets the company make it repeatedly.

Programme management here is less about tracking a plan than about building the
plan's scaffolding — deciding what has to be true before a date is credible, and
making the handoffs between engineering, sourcing and commercial visible enough
that nobody discovers a problem late.
